Regulatory & Engineering Standards

California Title 24 Roof/Wall R-Values & CBC §2603.4 Thermal Barriers

Commercial metal buildings, warehouses, and tenant retrofits in California rely heavily on 2.0 lb closed-cell spray polyurethane foam (ccSPF) to hit Title 24 Part 6 continuous insulation (ci) targets while simultaneously sealing air leakage in a single pass.

Under California Building Code (CBC) Section 2603.4, exposed foam plastic inside occupied commercial interiors MUST be separated from the interior by a 15-minute thermal barrier (typically 1/2-inch gypsum drywall) OR a tested, ICC-ES approved intumescent fire-retardant coating (such as DC315 or No-Burn Plus ThB) applied at the exact wet-mil thickness specified in its ESR report.

Code / Standard Requirement Jurisdiction Implementation
Title 24 Part 6 Commercial Roof / Wall R-Value Commercial metal roofs typically require R-19 to R-30 continuous insulation depending on California Climate Zone (CZ 1–16).
CBC §2603.4 15-Minute Thermal Barrier Occupied spaces require 1/2" drywall or ICC-ES NFPA 286 approved intumescent coating over exposed spray foam.
CBC §2603.4.1.6 Attic / Crawlspace Ignition Barrier Unoccupied mechanical attics/crawlspaces require a compliant ignition barrier coating if entered only for service.
Title 24 QII Insulation Depth Verification Third-party verification of uniform foam thickness and zero thermal bridging on metal purlins/girts.
Calculation Walkthrough

Commercial Spray Foam Board Foot & Drum Set Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Board Feet = Area (sq ft) × Thickness (in) × 1.10 (Waste/Texture) | 2.0 lb Closed-Cell Sets = Board Feet ÷ 4,000

Worked Example: 6,500 Sq Ft Commercial Metal Roof Deck in Fresno, CA

  • Step 1 — Target R-Value: 3.5 inches of 2.0 lb closed-cell SPF × R-6.5/in = R-22.8 continuous insulation + air/vapor barrier.
  • Step 2 — Board Feet (w/ 10% Waste): 6,500 sq ft × 3.5 in × 1.10 = 25,025 Board Feet.
  • Step 3 — Chemical Drum Sets: 25,025 BF ÷ 4,000 BF/set = 6.3 Sets (A-side + B-side 55-gal drums).
  • Step 4 — CBC §2603.4 Fire Coating: Exposed commercial ceiling requires ~18–20 wet mils of ICC-ES approved intumescent paint (e.g., DC315) over the foam.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — California — Title 24 & CBC

Can commercial spray foam be left exposed in a California warehouse?
Only if covered with an ICC-ES tested NFPA 286 intumescent thermal barrier coating (such as DC315) specifically approved for that exact spray foam product, or separated by 1/2-inch drywall per CBC §2603.4.
How many board feet are in one set of 2.0 lb closed-cell spray foam?
A standard 1,000 lb two-drum set (A + B) of 2.0 lb closed-cell foam theoretically yields ~4,000 to 4,500 board feet; commercial estimators use 4,000 BF per set to account for corrugated metal ribs and overspray.
What thickness of closed-cell spray foam gives R-20?
At approximately R-6.5 per inch, 3.0 to 3.25 inches of 2.0 lb closed-cell spray foam delivers R-19.5 to R-21 continuous insulation.
Does spray foam help pass California Title 24 blower door / air sealing requirements?
Yes. Closed-cell spray foam forms a monolithic air barrier at 1.5+ inches, dramatically reducing envelope air leakage compared to fiberglass batts.
