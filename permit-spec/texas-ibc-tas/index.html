Regulatory & Engineering Standards

The Texas TDLR TAS $50,000 Rule Every Commercial Contractor Must Know

Texas municipalities (Houston, Dallas, Austin, San Antonio) enforce the 2021 or 2024 International Building Code (IBC) and IECC energy code (via COMcheck). However, Texas enforces a unique statewide accessibility statute: the Architectural Barriers Act (Texas Government Code Chapter 469).

Any commercial construction, renovation, or tenant improvement in Texas with an estimated construction cost of $50,000 or more MUST be registered with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR), reviewed by a Registered Accessibility Specialist (RAS) before permitting, and inspected within 1 year of completion.

Calculation Walkthrough

Texas IBC Occupant Load & TI Budget Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Occupant Load = Area (sq ft) ÷ IBC Factor | TDLR TAS Trigger = Estimated Construction Cost >= $50,000

Worked Example: 5,000 Sq Ft Medical / Professional Office TI in Dallas, TX

  • Step 1 — Occupant Load: 5,000 sq ft ÷ 150 sq ft (Group B) = 34 Occupants.
  • Step 2 — Egress Check: Under 50 occupants, but confirm max common path of egress travel (<= 75 ft unsprinklered / <= 100 ft sprinklered).
  • Step 3 — Estimated TI Budget: 5,000 sq ft × $135/sq ft = $675,000.
  • Step 4 — TDLR TAS Filing: Exceeds $50,000 → obtain TDLR Project Number (TABS) and submit drawings to a Registered Accessibility Specialist (RAS).
Field Execution

Texas Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Register TABS Number With TDLR

If project valuation is >= $50,000, register online with TDLR ($175 state fee) and hire a Texas RAS before submitting to the city.

STEP 02

Generate COMcheck Energy Reports

Attach signed Lighting, Mechanical, and Envelope COMcheck compliance certificates to the permit package.

STEP 03

Verify Fire Marshal Sprinkler & Alarm Scope

Moving partitions or ceilings in sprinklered Texas buildings requires a licensed Texas RME-G / APS fire submittal.

STEP 04

Complete Post-Construction RAS Inspection

Schedule the mandatory RAS accessibility inspection within 365 days of substantial completion to avoid TDLR fines.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Texas — IBC & TDLR TAS

What triggers a TDLR TAS accessibility review in Texas?
Any commercial building construction, remodel, or tenant finish-out in Texas with an estimated construction cost of $50,000 or more requires TDLR registration and Registered Accessibility Specialist (RAS) review and inspection.
Do Texas cities require a TABS number before issuing a commercial building permit?
Yes. Houston, Dallas, Austin, and San Antonio building departments require your TDLR Architectural Barriers (TABS) registration number on the permit application for projects >= $50,000.
What is the IBC occupant load factor for a commercial office in Texas?
Under the 2021 and 2024 IBC Table 1004.5, Business areas (offices) are calculated at 150 gross square feet per occupant (updated from the older 100 sq ft rule).
Is an architect seal required for a commercial tenant improvement in Texas?
Under the Texas Board of Architectural Examiners (TBAE), an architect seal is generally required for commercial alterations exceeding $50,000 or involving life-safety/egress changes.
