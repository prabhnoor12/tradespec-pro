Hidden California TI Budget Triggers: Title 24 & Chapter 11B Path of Travel

Commercial Tenant Improvements (TI) in Los Angeles, San Francisco, San Diego, and Orange County carry two major regulatory cost drivers beyond standard IBC jurisdictions: California Title 24 Part 6 Energy Standards (requiring automated lighting controls, demand-responsive thermostats, and HERS/ATTCP acceptance testing) and CBC Chapter 11B Disabled Access.

Under CBC §11B-202.4, if your TI valuation is below the annual DSA valuation threshold (approx. $200,000+), you must spend up to 20% of the adjusted construction cost upgrading the accessible path of travel (parking, entrance, restrooms, drinking fountains). Above the threshold, full 100% path-of-travel compliance is triggered.

Code / Standard Requirement Jurisdiction Implementation
CBC Table 1004.5 Occupant Load Factors Business/Office: 150 gross sq ft; Mercantile/Retail: 60 gross sq ft; Assembly (Unconcentrated): 15 net sq ft.
CBC §1006.2.1 Two-Exit Threshold Spaces with occupant load >= 50 (Group B, M, A) or common path of egress > 75–100 ft require 2 remote exits.
CBC §11B-202.4 20% Path-of-Travel Rule Alterations trigger mandatory accessible parking, entry, and restroom upgrades up to 20% of project valuation (or full compliance above DSA threshold).
Title 24 Part 6 NRCC Lighting & Mechanical Requires certified Acceptance Test Technician (ATTCP) sign-off on commercial lighting controls and HVAC economizers.
Calculation Walkthrough

CBC Occupant Load & Chapter 11B Budget Reserve Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Occupant Load = Gross Sq Ft ÷ CBC Table 1004.5 Factor | ADA Reserve = 20% × TI Valuation (Below DSA Cap)

Worked Example: 4,000 Sq Ft Commercial Office TI in Orange County, CA

  • Step 1 — Occupant Load (Group B): 4,000 sq ft ÷ 150 sq ft/occupant = 27 Occupants (1 exit permitted if travel distance <= 100 ft sprinklered).
  • Step 2 — Base TI Construction Cost: 4,000 sq ft × $185/sq ft = $740,000.
  • Step 3 — CBC Chapter 11B Trigger: Exceeds annual DSA valuation threshold (~$200k) → full accessible path-of-travel compliance (parking stall, entry door pressure <= 5 lbf, compliant restroom).
  • Step 4 — Title 24 Acceptance Testing: Budget $3,500–$6,000 for certified ATTCP lighting & mechanical acceptance testing prior to final inspection.
Field Execution

California Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Commission a CASp Inspection Before Signing Lease

A Certified Access Specialist (CASp) report identifies hidden parking/restroom barriers so you can negotiate landlord TI allowance.

STEP 02

Verify Change of Occupancy (e.g., Retail → Restaurant)

Changing occupancy group (M to A-2 or B) triggers full seismic, fire sprinkler, and Title 24 upgrades.

STEP 03

Include Title 24 NRCC Forms in Plan Submittal

Architectural, mechanical, and electrical Title 24 energy compliance forms must be stamped onto the permit drawing set.

STEP 04

Schedule ATTCP Acceptance Testing

City inspectors will not grant final Certificate of Occupancy without signed Title 24 NRCA acceptance certificates.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — California — CBC & Title 24

What is the 20% ADA rule for California commercial tenant improvements?
Under CBC §11B-202.4, when a commercial TI project is below the state's annual DSA valuation threshold (approx. $200,000+), the owner must spend up to 20% of the construction budget upgrading accessible path-of-travel elements (entrance, restrooms, parking).
When do I need 2 exits in a California commercial space?
Under CBC Table 1006.2.1, Business (Group B), Mercantile (Group M), and Assembly (Group A) spaces require at least 2 exits whenever the occupant load reaches 50 or more (or if common path of egress exceeds code limits).
What is Title 24 ATTCP acceptance testing?
California Title 24 Part 6 requires an independent, state-certified Acceptance Test Technician to functionally test commercial lighting controls (occupancy sensors, daylight dimming) and HVAC systems before final inspection.
How long do commercial TI permits take in Los Angeles or San Francisco?
Simple office TIs can clear over-the-counter or in 4–8 weeks; full restaurant or change-of-use TIs typically require 3 to 6 months across Building, Health, and Fire plan checks.
