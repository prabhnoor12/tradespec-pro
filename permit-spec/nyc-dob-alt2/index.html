Regulatory & Engineering Standards

NYC DOB Alt-2 vs. Alt-1 Filings & Special Inspections (TR1/TR8)

Commercial buildouts in New York City require strict classification under NYC DOB rules. Standard office or retail interior renovations that do not change the building's use, egress, or occupancy group file as an Alteration (formerly Alt-2) in DOB NOW—often eligible for Professional Certification by a NY Registered Architect (RA) or PE for faster approval.

Projects that change the occupancy group (for example, converting retail Group M into a restaurant Group A-2 over 74 occupants) require a full Alteration-CO (formerly Alt-1) resulting in a new Certificate of Occupancy, plus FDNY fire alarm and sprinkler sign-offs.

Occupant Load = Sq Ft ÷ NYC BC Table 1004.5 | If Assembly >= 75 Persons → Place of Assembly (PA) Permit Required

Worked Example: 3,500 Sq Ft Commercial Office Suite in Midtown Manhattan

  • Step 1 — Occupant Load: 3,500 sq ft ÷ 150 sq ft (Group B) = 24 Occupants.
  • Step 2 — Filing Classification: Existing Group B office remaining Group B → qualifies for DOB NOW Alteration (Alt-2).
  • Step 3 — Estimated NYC TI Budget: 3,500 sq ft × $240/sq ft = $840,000 (includes union/building-approved MEP & expediting).
  • Step 4 — Asbestos & Special Inspections: File mandatory NYC DEP ACP-5 asbestos form prior to DOB permit issuance plus TR1/TR8 energy inspections.
Field Execution

New York City Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Obtain NYC DEP ACP-5 Form First

A certified NYC Asbestos Investigator must issue an ACP-5 (No Asbestos) form before DOB will issue any alteration permit.

STEP 02

Verify Existing Certificate of Occupancy

Confirm current legal use group on NYC DOB BIS/DOB NOW to avoid triggering an unexpected Alt-1 CO change.

STEP 03

Hire Special Inspection Agency (TR1 / TR8)

Firestopping, mechanical, sprinkler, and NYCECC energy code inspections require a DOB-registered Special Inspection Agency.

STEP 04

Confirm Building Class A Rules

Verify landlord union labor rules, freight elevator hours, and Certificate of Insurance (COI) limits before bidding.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — NYC — BC 2022 & DOB Alt-2

What is the difference between a NYC DOB Alt-1 and Alt-2 permit?
An Alt-2 (Alteration) covers interior renovations (partitions, plumbing, HVAC, electrical) that do not change the building's use, egress, or occupancy. An Alt-1 (Alteration-CO) is required when changing occupancy group or egress, resulting in a new Certificate of Occupancy.
When is a NYC Place of Assembly (PA) permit required?
Any indoor commercial space (restaurant, bar, event venue, conference center) with a calculated occupancy of 75 or more persons requires a DOB Place of Assembly Certificate of Operation.
Do I need an ACP-5 asbestos form for a modern NYC office buildout?
Yes. Unless exempt under specific DOB criteria, an ACP-5 signed by a DEP-certified Asbestos Investigator is required for virtually all NYC commercial alteration filings.
Can a contractor pull a commercial TI permit in NYC without an architect?
No. Commercial interior alterations in NYC must be filed by a New York State Registered Architect (RA) or Professional Engineer (PE) on DOB NOW.
