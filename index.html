Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on calculation accuracy, engineering stamp requirements, and licensing.

Are TradeSpec Pro calculators officially certified?

TradeSpec Pro tools implement the exact mathematical and physical relationships dictated by ASME B30.5, OSHA 1926/1910, RMI ANSI MH16.1, ASHRAE 90.1, and UPC standards. They are engineered as preliminary planning, bidding, and budgeting instruments. Final structural plans, tandem picks, or critical public installations must receive written stamp and approval by a licensed Professional Engineer (PE).

Can I export calculation results to a customer quote or permit application?

Yes. Each sub-tool includes a dedicated "Print / Export Engineering Worksheet" button. The stylesheet optimizes the worksheet into clean, ad-free 8.5x11 inch pages suitable for customer estimates, submittal packets, and municipal permit reviews.

How does TradeSpec Pro monetize if the tools are free to use?

TradeSpec Pro operates on a high-value commercial lead generation and supplier referral model. When a general contractor sizes a 75-ton crane pick or a 500-foot well, they frequently need bids from licensed local operators. We connect them with certified regional contractors who pay a modest qualification fee for commercial project inquiries.

Can I embed or bookmark individual calculators separately?

Absolutely. Each calculator is completely standalone, fully functional on mobile and desktop, and hosted under its own subpath (e.g., /crane-lift/, /well-drilling/). You can bookmark direct URLs on field tablets or smartphone home screens.
