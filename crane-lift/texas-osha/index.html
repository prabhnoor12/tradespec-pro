Regulatory & Engineering Standards

Why Texas Crane Picks Require Larger Outrigger Cribbing & Strict Wind Planning

Texas is a Federal OSHA jurisdiction for private commercial construction, meaning every mobile crane lift in Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, and San Antonio is governed by OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart CC (1926.1400–1442) alongside ASME B30.5. Under 1926.1402, the controlling entity—typically the general contractor or property owner—is legally responsible for verifying ground conditions before the crane sets up.

In the Gulf Coast and North Texas corridors, high-plasticity expansive clay (Beaumont clay in Houston, Eagle Ford clay in DFW) frequently tests at just 1,500 to 2,000 PSF allowable bearing pressure after rain. Placing an undersized 4 ft × 4 ft pad under a 90-ton crane outrigger can punch through asphalt or cause differential settlement during a boom swing.

Calculation Walkthrough

Outrigger Mat Area & Gross Pick Formula (Texas Clay Default)

Governing Engineering Formula:
Gross Load = Net Weight × 1.15 (Rigging) | Min Mat Area (sq ft) = Corner Reaction (lbs) ÷ Soil PSF

Worked Example: 14,000 lb Rooftop Chiller Pick in Houston, TX

  • Step 1 — Gross Pick Weight: 14,000 lb chiller + 2,100 lb spreader bar, slings, and hook block (15% rigging allowance) = 16,100 lbs gross.
  • Step 2 — Radius & Crane Class: 60 ft horizontal setback from curb to roof dunnage requires a 85-Ton to 100-Ton hydraulic crane to stay under the 75% non-critical chart limit.
  • Step 3 — Outrigger Corner Reaction: Peak swing reaction over one corner float ≈ 78,000 lbs.
  • Step 4 — Houston Clay Mat Sizing: At 1,800 PSF allowable bearing pressure, 78,000 ÷ 1,800 = 43.3 sq ft (approx. 6.6 ft × 6.6 ft engineered composite or layered hardwood crane mats).
Field Execution

Texas Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Verify Controlling Entity Ground Sign-Off

Check for underground detention vaults, sanitary sewer lines, and soft clay shoulders before crane arrival per OSHA 1926.1402.

STEP 02

File Municipal ROW / Street Closure

City of Houston Mobility Permit or City of Dallas Right-of-Way permit requires a stamped Traffic Control Plan (TCP) 5–10 business days ahead.

STEP 03

Confirm Utility Clearances

Coordinate with CenterPoint Energy or Oncor if boom swing approaches overhead distribution feeders within 20 feet.

STEP 04

Monitor Gulf Coast Gust Limits

Suspend picks if sustained winds or gusts exceed manufacturer chart derating limits (typically 20–25 mph for high-sail-area HVAC units).

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Texas — OSHA 1926 & TxDOT

Does Texas have its own state crane license or does it follow Federal OSHA?
Texas does not issue a separate state crane operator license. Private commercial lifts follow Federal OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1427, which requires nationally accredited certification (such as NCCCO or NCCER) for the specific crane type.
What soil bearing pressure should I use for outrigger mats in Houston or Dallas?
Unless a geotechnical report proves otherwise, conservative riggers use 1,500 to 2,000 PSF for wet Gulf Coast or North Texas clay, and 2,500 to 3,000 PSF for compacted crushed limestone base.
Do I need a TxDOT permit to drive a 90-ton crane to a Texas jobsite?
Yes. Most 60-ton and larger hydraulic cranes exceed standard 80,000 lb gross vehicle weight or axle weight limits and require a TxDOT ProMiles oversize/overweight permit.
How far in advance do I need a street closure permit for a crane in Austin or Houston?
Plan for 5 to 14 business days for a municipal Right-of-Way / Traffic Control Plan (TCP) approval when closing a travel lane or sidewalk.
