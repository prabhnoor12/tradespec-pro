Regulatory & Engineering Standards

How Cal/OSHA Title 8 Differs From Federal OSHA on Mobile Crane Picks

California operates an independent State Plan under Cal/OSHA Title 8, Group 13 (Cranes and Other Hoisting Equipment, §§4884–5049) that enforces stricter certification and inspection standards than Federal OSHA 1926 Subpart CC. Every mobile crane exceeding 3 tons rated capacity operating in California must hold a current annual certificate from a Cal/OSHA-accredited certifying agency.

Urban jobsites in Los Angeles, San Jose, and San Francisco also face tight alley setbacks, subterranean parking structures, and strict municipal noise and lane-closure windows (LADOT Peak Hour Exemptions, SFMTA Special Traffic Permits).

Code / Standard Requirement Jurisdiction Implementation
Cal/OSHA §5021 Mandatory Crane Certification Annual & quadrennial certification by a Cal/OSHA-accredited certifier is mandatory for cranes over 3 tons capacity.
Cal/OSHA §5006.1 Operator Qualification Operators must hold valid accredited certification plus physical exam (ASME B30.5 medical requirements) and substance testing.
Cal/OSHA §4994 Hoisting & Ground Stability Outriggers must be fully extended per chart; cribbing required to distribute load over soft alluvial soils or subterranean slabs.
Cal/OSHA §2946 High-Voltage Clearance Strict minimum approach distances from PG&E, SCE, and LADWP energized overhead lines (10–20+ ft based on voltage).
Caltrans / LADOT Peak Hour & Haul Permits Oversize crane mobilization requires Caltrans Transportation Permit plus local Peak Hour Exemption for arterial street closures.
Calculation Walkthrough

Cal/OSHA Pick Margin & Outrigger Bearing Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Gross Pick = Net Load × 1.18 (CA Rigging/Headache Ball) | Mat Area = Corner Load ÷ Soil PSF

Worked Example: 10,000 lb Commercial Air Handler Pick in Los Angeles, CA

  • Step 1 — Gross Pick Weight: 10,000 lb AHU + 1,800 lb rigging gear, slings, and hook block = 11,800 lbs gross.
  • Step 2 — Radius & Crane Class: 55 ft radius over a 2-story retail parapet requires a 60-Ton to 75-Ton hydraulic crane with jib or main boom clearance.
  • Step 3 — Outrigger Corner Reaction: Peak swing force ≈ 58,500 lbs on the load-side outrigger.
  • Step 4 — Mat Sizing: On 2,200 PSF compacted SoCal soil, 58,500 ÷ 2,200 = 26.6 sq ft (minimum 5.2 ft × 5.2 ft cribbing pads).

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — California — Cal/OSHA Title 8

Is Federal OSHA NCCCO certification valid in California?
Yes, NCCCO and other nationally accredited certifications meet Cal/OSHA §5006.1, provided the operator also maintains current ASME B30.5 medical and substance-testing compliance.
Does California require a state inspection sticker on every mobile crane?
Yes. Under Cal/OSHA Title 8 §5021, mobile cranes over 3 tons capacity must be inspected and certified annually by a Cal/OSHA-accredited certifying agency.
Can I set crane outriggers over an underground parking garage in Los Angeles?
Never without a licensed California Structural Engineer (SE/PE) shoring analysis. Outrigger corner reactions easily exceed 50,000–100,000 lbs and will punch through post-tensioned slabs.
How long does a Los Angeles crane street closure permit take?
LADOT and Bureau of Street Services (BSS) permits typically require 10 to 15 business days, especially if a Peak Hour Exemption or Metro bus reroute is involved.
