Regulatory & Engineering Standards

NYC DEP Grease Interceptor Standards for Dense Urban Kitchens

Because almost all New York City restaurants operate in ground-floor retail spaces without parking lots, exterior concrete gravity vaults are rarely feasible. Instead, the 2022 NYC Plumbing Code (Section 1003.3) and NYC Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) rely heavily on properly sized indoor Hydromechanical Grease Interceptors (HGIs) certified to PDI G101 or ASME A112.14.3.

NYC DEP inspectors routinely issue hefty fines when kitchens omit the mandatory vented flow-control device, undersize the trap relative to 3-compartment sink volume, or fail to maintain grease cleaning logs.

Code / Standard Requirement Jurisdiction Implementation
NYC PC §1003.3.4 PDI G101 / ASME A112.14.3 Rating Indoor grease traps must match total connected fixture flow rate in GPM with 2× lbs grease retention capacity.
NYC PC §1003.3.4.2 Mandatory Flow Control Device Every hydromechanical interceptor must have an accessible, vented flow-control fitting upstream of the trap.
NYC DEP BMP Weekly / Monthly Cleaning Logs NYC DEP requires regular skimming/cleaning before 25% capacity and visible maintenance logs posted on site.
NYC PC §1003.3.2 No Food Waste Grinders Commercial garbage disposals discharging to grease interceptors are strictly prohibited in NYC.
Calculation Walkthrough

NYC Plumbing Code Fixture-Volume GPM Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Required GPM = (Total Sink Compartment Cubic Inches ÷ 231) × 0.75 ÷ 1-Min Drain | Retention = GPM × 2 lbs

Worked Example: 65-Seat Bistro Kitchen in Brooklyn, NY

  • Step 1 — Calculate Sink Volume: 3-compartment pot sink (16" × 16" × 14" each) = 15.5 gal × 3 = 46.5 gallons.
  • Step 2 — Apply 75% Load Factor: 46.5 gal × 0.75 = 34.9 GPM.
  • Step 3 — Select Standard PDI Size: Round up to a 35 GPM (70 lb) or 50 GPM (100 lb) PDI-certified hydromechanical grease interceptor.
  • Step 4 — Flow Control & Access: Install vented flow control tee and ensure minimum 18-inch vertical clearance above lid for weekly baffle removal.
Field Execution

New York City Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

File Plumbing Alteration (DOB NOW)

Licensed NYC Master Plumber (LMP) must file grease interceptor installation on DOB NOW plumbing drawings.

STEP 02

Verify Flow Control Venting

Confirm the external flow control device has an air intake vent installed per manufacturer and NYC PC §1003.

STEP 03

Install Solids Strainer Basket

Add removable mesh baskets on 3-compartment sinks to prevent food solids from choking the HGI.

STEP 04

Maintain NYC DEP Logbook

Log every cleaning date, volume removed, and licensed grease hauler manifest to avoid NYC DEP NOV fines.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — NYC — Plumbing Code & DEP

What is the smallest legal commercial grease trap in NYC?
Under NYC Plumbing Code §1003.3.4, commercial hydromechanical grease interceptors must have a minimum flow rating of 20 GPM (40 lbs grease retention capacity).
Can I connect a commercial dishwasher to a grease trap in NYC?
No. NYC DEP and NYC Plumbing Code prohibit connecting commercial dishwashers (which use hot water >140°F and detergents) to indoor hydromechanical grease traps.
Do I need a Licensed Master Plumber (LMP) to install a grease trap in NYC?
Yes. Any alteration to commercial sanitary drainage piping in NYC must be performed and signed off by a NYC Licensed Master Plumber.
What happens if NYC DEP inspects and finds no flow control device?
NYC DEP issues a Notice of Violation (NOV) requiring immediate retrofit of a vented flow control fitting, as unthrottled sink drainage flushes emulsified grease straight into the city sewer.
