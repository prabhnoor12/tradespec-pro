### The Restaurant Owner's Nightmare
When opening or renovating a restaurant, coffee shop, or ghost kitchen, health departments and municipal wastewater districts mandate exact sizing for **Grease Interceptors (FOG: Fats, Oils & Grease)** and **Type I Kitchen Exhaust Hoods**.

## ⚙️ Engineering & Calculation Model

1. **UPC Liquid Fixture Surge:**
   Calculates total liquid capacity across 3-compartment pot wash sinks ($\frac{\text{Cubic Inches}}{231} \times 0.75$), dishwashers, and food prep sinks. Converts to required Gallons Per Minute (GPM).
2. **Grease Retention Capacity (Lbs):**
   Grounded in standard plumbing ratings: $\text{Grease Retention Rating (lbs)} = 2 \times \text{GPM Flow Rate}$.
3. **NFPA 96 Airflow Velocity (CFM):**
   Calculates minimum exhaust airflow per linear foot of canopy hood based on cooking appliance thermal classification (Light Duty: 225 CFM/ft, Medium: 300 CFM/ft, Heavy Charbroiler: 400 CFM/ft, Solid Fuel: 550 CFM/ft).
4. **Make-Up Air (MUA) Balancing:**
   Enforces an 85% tempered fresh air supply volume to eliminate building negative air pressure locks.
