Regulatory & Engineering Standards

Florida Building Code & County-Level FOG Rules (Miami-Dade DERM)

The Florida Building Code (FBC) – Plumbing follows the IPC framework in Section 1003, but county utility departments (such as Miami-Dade DERM, Broward County, Orange County Utilities, and Hillsborough County) enforce strict minimum exterior vault sizes—commonly 750 to 1,250 gallons minimum for any full-service Food Service Establishment (FSE).

A critical Florida-specific engineering factor is buoyancy: because groundwater sits just 2 to 4 feet below grade in South and Central Florida, buried fiberglass or concrete grease interceptors must be engineered with anti-flotation concrete hold-down slabs so the empty tank doesn't pop out of the ground during pump-out!

Florida Gravity Interceptor & Buoyancy Check Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
GGI Size = max(750 gal, Seats × Meals/Hr × Waste Factor × Retention) | Check Anti-Flotation for High Water Table

Worked Example: 110-Seat Restaurant in Miami-Dade County, FL

  • Step 1 — Kitchen Flow Assessment: 3-compartment sink (60 gal total) + wok/prep sinks + 110 dining seats.
  • Step 2 — Gravity Vault Sizing: 110 seats × 12 gal/seat baseline = 1,320 gal → 1,500-Gallon exterior precast concrete grease interceptor.
  • Step 3 — Anti-Flotation Engineering: Spec heavy-wall precast concrete vault (or strapped fiberglass tank on concrete ballast pad) rated for water table at finished grade.
  • Step 4 — DERM GDO Permit: Apply for Miami-Dade DERM Grease Discharge Operating Permit prior to Certificate of Occupancy.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Florida / Miami-Dade — FBC & DERM

What is the minimum outdoor grease interceptor size in Miami-Dade County?
Miami-Dade DERM typically requires a minimum 750 to 1,250-gallon exterior gravity grease interceptor for full-service restaurants, depending on seat count and menu grease intensity.
Why do buried grease traps float in Florida?
Florida's shallow groundwater table exerts massive upward hydrostatic pressure when a tank is pumped empty during maintenance. Concrete ballast slabs or heavy precast concrete vaults prevent tank flotation.
Can I use an under-sink grease trap in a Florida strip mall restaurant?
Only if the local county utility approves a hardship variance (typically for low-grease coffee shops or sandwich concepts without fryers/grills) using a high-capacity PDI/ASME hydromechanical unit.
Do I need a separate DERM permit in addition to my building permit?
Yes. In Miami-Dade County, food service establishments must obtain a DERM Grease Discharge Operating (GDO) permit and renew it annually.
