## ⚙️ Engineering & Calculation Model

1. **Total Dynamic Head (TDH):**
   $$\text{TDH} = \text{Pumping Drawdown Lift (ft)} + \text{Pressure Head (138.6 ft at 60 PSI)} + \text{Friction Loss}$$
2. **Hydraulic & Electrical Pump Sizing:**
   Calculates water horsepower ($WHP = \frac{GPM \times TDH}{3960}$) and sizes 4-inch deep-well submersible motors from 0.5 HP to 3.0 HP.
3. **Pressure Tank Drawdown Volume:**
   Calculates minimum gallon drawdown ($V_{drawdown} \ge 1.5 \times GPM$) to enforce a 90-second minimum runtime and prevent motor burn-out.
4. **Itemized Turnkey Costing:**
   Breaks down rotary drilling footage, sanitary casing, annular grout seals, pitless adapter frost protection, trenching, pressure tank controls, and DNR/health department water tests.
