Regulatory & Engineering Standards

Tapping the Floridan Aquifer Under FDEP Chapter 62-532 & WMD Rules

Commercial, irrigation, and industrial wells across Central and North Florida typically tap the high-yielding Upper Floridan limestone aquifer (often 200 to 650 feet deep), while South Florida commercial wells tap either the surficial Biscayne Aquifer or deeper brackish Floridan zones. All well construction is governed by FDEP Chapter 62-532 F.A.C. and delegated to Florida's five Water Management Districts.

Commercial wells with casing diameters of 6 inches or larger—or average daily withdrawals exceeding district thresholds (often 100,000 GPD)—require a formal Water Use Permit (WUP) or Consumptive Use Permit (CUP) alongside the standard well construction permit.

Calculation Walkthrough

Florida Limestone Aquifer TDH & Pump Sizing Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
TDH (ft) = Pumping Level + (50 PSI × 2.31) + Friction | HP = (GPM × TDH) ÷ (3,960 × 0.62)

Worked Example: 350 ft, 75 GPM Upper Floridan Commercial Well in Central FL

  • Step 1 — Pumping Level & TDH: High-yield Floridan limestone well with 252 ft pumping head + 125 ft surface pressure/friction = 377 ft TDH.
  • Step 2 — Pump Horsepower: (75 GPM × 377 ft) ÷ 2,455 = 11.5 → specify 15 HP submersible pump with stainless steel impellers for mineral resistance.
  • Step 3 — Casing & Grout: 6-inch Schedule 80 PVC or steel casing grouted solidly into the Ocala/Avon Park limestone cap.
  • Step 4 — Turnkey Budget: 350 ft × $52/ft ($18,200) + 15 HP pump/VFD/wellhead ($18,750) ≈ $36,950 turnkey.
Field Execution

Florida Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Verify WMD CUP / WUP Threshold

Check SFWMD, SWFWMD, or SJRWMD rules to see if your casing diameter (>= 6") or daily GPM triggers a Consumptive Use Permit.

STEP 02

Screen for Saltwater Intrusion Zones

Coastal Florida parcels require chloride testing and strict well-depth caps to avoid brackish upconing.

STEP 03

Confirm Septic & Stormwater Setbacks

Maintain minimum 75–100 ft separation from septic systems and sanitary hazards per FDEP 62-532.

STEP 04

File Well Completion Report

Licensed Florida Water Well Contractor must submit completion log and cuttings data within 30 days.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Florida — FDEP & WMD CUP

When do I need a Consumptive Use Permit (CUP) for a commercial well in Florida?
Most Florida Water Management Districts require a CUP/WUP when a commercial well uses a 6-inch or larger casing diameter, withdraws over 100,000 gallons per day, or sits inside a Water Use Caution Area.
How deep are commercial Floridan Aquifer wells in Central Florida?
Most commercial Upper Floridan wells in Orlando, Tampa, and Jacksonville range from 250 to 600 feet deep, frequently yielding 60 to 300+ GPM.
Does Florida require a licensed driller for commercial wells?
Yes. Under Florida Statutes Chapter 373, all water well construction must be performed by a Water Management District-licensed Water Well Contractor.
Why is stainless steel recommended for Florida commercial well pumps?
Floridan Aquifer water often contains elevated dissolved sulfur (hydrogen sulfide), iron, and chlorides in coastal areas, which corrode cast-iron pump housings rapidly.
