Regulatory & Engineering Standards

California SGMA Permitting & DWR Bulletin 74-90 Sanitary Seal Rules

Drilling a commercial or agricultural water well in California requires navigating both County Environmental Health Department (EHD) construction standards (DWR Bulletin 74-90) and the Sustainable Groundwater Management Act (SGMA). In medium- and high-priority basins across the Central Valley, Salinas Valley, and Southern California, counties must obtain written consistency verification from the local Groundwater Sustainability Agency (GSA) before issuing a commercial well permit.

California also mandates a much deeper annular sanitary cement seal—typically 50 feet minimum for commercial/agricultural wells (and 50–100+ feet for public supply)—plus a CSLB C-57 licensed drilling contractor.

Code / Standard Requirement Jurisdiction Implementation
DWR Bulletin 74-90 50-Foot Annular Sanitary Seal Commercial and public supply wells require a minimum 50 ft cement grout seal inspected by County EHD.
SGMA / Exec. Order GSA Consistency Verification New non-domestic wells in medium/high-priority basins require written GSA determination of no undesirable subsidence/depletion.
CSLB C-57 License Licensed Well Driller All drilling, deepening, and well destruction must be performed by an active California C-57 contractor.
SWRCB DDW Public Supply Standards Commercial facilities serving 25+ people/day trigger State Water Board Division of Drinking Water permitting.
Calculation Walkthrough

California Deep-Well TDH & Submersible Pump Sizing Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
TDH (ft) = Pumping Depth + 115 ft (50 PSI) + Friction | HP = (GPM × TDH) ÷ (3,960 × 0.62)

Worked Example: 600 ft, 80 GPM Commercial / Ag Well in Central California

  • Step 1 — Pumping Level & TDH: 600 ft alluvial basin well with 432 ft pumping water level + 125 ft pressure/friction = 557 ft TDH.
  • Step 2 — Pump Horsepower: (80 GPM × 557 ft) ÷ 2,455 = 18.15 → specify 20 HP 480V 3-phase submersible pump with VFD.
  • Step 3 — Casing & 50 ft Seal: 6-inch or 8-inch stainless-screened casing with 50 ft neat-cement sanitary seal witnessed by County EHD.
  • Step 4 — Turnkey Budget: 600 ft × $95/ft ($57,000) + 20 HP pump/VFD/column ($23,500) ≈ $80,500 turnkey.
Field Execution

California Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Obtain Local SGMA GSA Written Verification

Verify basin priority and obtain GSA groundwater sustainability consistency sign-off prior to County EHD submittal.

STEP 02

Hire Active CSLB C-57 Contractor

Verify C-57 license and workers' comp before filing the County Environmental Health well permit.

STEP 03

Schedule Witnessed 50-Foot Annular Seal

County EHD inspector must witness the cement grout pour for the minimum 50-foot sanitary seal.

STEP 04

Install Totalizing Flow Meter

Most California GSAs require calibrated flow meters and semi-annual groundwater extraction reporting.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — California — DWR & SGMA

How deep is the required sanitary seal for a commercial well in California?
Under DWR Bulletin 74-90, agricultural and commercial wells typically require a minimum 50-foot annular cement seal (compared to 20 feet for standard domestic wells in some counties).
How does SGMA affect new commercial water wells in California?
Under the Sustainable Groundwater Management Act (SGMA), counties cannot issue permits for new non-domestic wells in medium- or high-priority basins without verifying consistency with the local GSA's Groundwater Sustainability Plan.
What license is required to drill a water well in California?
Only a contractor holding an active California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) C-57 Water Well Drilling license can legally drill or rehabilitate a water well.
How much does a commercial water well cost in California?
Expect $75 to $130+ per foot for commercial 6-inch to 10-inch rotary-drilled wells with stainless steel screen, gravel pack, and 50-foot cement seal, excluding pump and electrical service.
