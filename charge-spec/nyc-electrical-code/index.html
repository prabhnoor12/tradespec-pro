Designing NYC Commercial Garage EV Charging With Con Edison PowerReady

New York City parking garages face tight electrical room space, older 208V 3-phase services, and strict NYC DOB electrical permitting. Con Edison's PowerReady program offsets 50% to 100% of utility-side and customer-side make-ready electrical costs (transformers, conduit, switchgear) for qualifying commercial Level 2 and DC Fast Charging sites.

Because upgrading a Manhattan building service can take 12+ months, using UL-listed EMS load management (Article 625.42) to share existing 208V/480V spare capacity is the fastest path to commissioning.

Calculation Walkthrough

NYC Garage 208V/480V EV Load Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Design kW = (Ports × kW × EMS Sharing) × 1.25 | Amps (480V 3Ø) = Design kW × 1,000 ÷ 831.4

Worked Example: 12-Charger Commercial Parking Garage in Queens, NY

  • Step 1 — Connected Load: 12 commercial Level 2 ports × 11.5 kW (48A) = 138 kW raw load.
  • Step 2 — EMS 2:1 Load Management: Dynamic load sharing limits total draw to 69 kW.
  • Step 3 — 125% Continuous Sizing: 69 kW × 1.25 = 86.3 kW continuous (104 Amps @ 480V 3Ø or 240 Amps @ 208V 3Ø).
  • Step 4 — Con Edison PowerReady: Submit single-line diagram and Licensed Master Electrician quote to Con Edison PowerReady portal prior to construction.

New York City Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Enroll in Con Edison PowerReady Before Construction

Incentive approval must be secured prior to pulling conduit or installing switchgear.

STEP 02

Verify 208V vs. 480V Building Service

Many older NYC commercial garages operate on 208V/120V 3-phase; verify voltage drop and panel busbar rating.

File NYC DOB Electrical Permit

Only a NYC Licensed Master Electrician (LME) can file ED16A/DOB NOW electrical permits.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — NYC — Electrical Code & ConEd

How much does Con Edison PowerReady pay for commercial EV installations?
Depending on charger type, public accessibility, and disadvantaged community (DAC) location, Con Edison PowerReady can reimburse 50% to 100% of eligible make-ready electrical infrastructure costs.
Do NYC parking garages need a new Con Edison service to add EV chargers?
Not always. If a 30-day load study shows spare capacity on the existing switchgear, UL-listed Energy Management Systems (EMS) allow adding 10–30+ Level 2 ports without waiting for a utility service upgrade.
What permits are needed for commercial EV chargers in NYC?
A NYC DOB Electrical Permit filed by a Licensed Master Electrician is mandatory; larger switchgear or structural trenching work also requires DOB Alt-2 filings.
Are NYC commercial EV chargers required to be networked?
To qualify for Con Edison PowerReady or NYSERDA Charge Ready NY 2.0 incentives, chargers must be networked (OCPP/cellular) and share utilization data.
