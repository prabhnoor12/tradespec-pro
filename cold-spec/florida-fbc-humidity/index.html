Regulatory & Engineering Standards

Why Latent Humidity Dominates Walk-In Freezer Loads in Florida

In Miami, Tampa, Orlando, and Jacksonville, ambient relative humidity regularly sits at 75% to 90% with dew points near 78°F. Every time a kitchen staff member opens a walk-in freezer door, warm moisture-laden air rushes inside and instantly condenses and freezes on the evaporator coil—creating massive latent heat load and rapid coil icing.

Under the Florida Building Code – Energy Conservation, walk-in boxes require automatic door closers, heavy-duty vinyl strip curtains or air curtains, and properly sized electric/hot-gas defrost cycles. Coastal installations also require E-coated or Heresite-coated condenser coils to survive salt-air corrosion.

Calculation Walkthrough

Florida High-Latent-Load Walk-In Sizing Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Total BTU/hr = Floor Sq Ft × Base Factor × (ΔT ÷ 60) × 1.25 (High Humidity / Latent Factor)

Worked Example: 360 Sq Ft Walk-In Freezer (0°F) in Miami, FL

  • Step 1 — Box & Ambient ΔT: 0°F freezer with 95°F ambient → ΔT = 95°F.
  • Step 2 — High-Latent Load Calculation: 360 sq ft × 48 BTU/sq ft × (95 ÷ 60) × 1.25 = 34,200 BTU/hr (2.85 Tons).
  • Step 3 — Condensing Unit Selection: Select a 4.0 HP low-temp condensing unit with factory E-coated coil and electric defrost evaporator.
  • Step 4 — Moisture Control: Install heavy-duty strip curtains, heated pressure relief port, and heat-traced condensate drain line.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Florida — FBC & Latent Humidity

Why do walk-in freezers ice up so fast in Florida?
Florida's high ambient dew point means every door opening pulls heavy moisture into the box. Without tight gaskets, strip curtains, and 4 daily defrost cycles, frost rapidly blocks evaporator airflow.
What coil coating is recommended for commercial refrigeration in Florida?
Factory-applied E-coat (electrocoating) or phenolic/Heresite coatings protect aluminum condenser fins from coastal salt-spray corrosion.
Do outdoor walk-in coolers in South Florida need Miami-Dade NOA approval?
Yes. Outdoor walk-in coolers/freezers installed in the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone (Miami-Dade and Broward) require structural wind-load engineering and anchored tie-downs per FBC HVHZ.
How much extra BTU capacity should I add for Florida humidity?
For high-traffic commercial kitchens in humid coastal Florida, adding a 15% to 25% latent infiltration allowance prevents under-sizing during peak summer humidity.
