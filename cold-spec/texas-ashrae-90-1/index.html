Regulatory & Engineering Standards

Why Standard 95°F Refrigeration Charts Fail on Texas Rooftops

Most manufacturer walk-in condensing unit brochures publish headline BTU ratings at 90°F or 95°F ambient air temperature. In Texas, ASHRAE 0.4% cooling design temperatures exceed 100°F–103°F—and black TPO or gravel commercial rooftops routinely hit 110°F–115°F in July and August.

At 105°F–110°F ambient, an air-cooled condensing unit loses 12% to 20% of its rated BTU capacity while head pressures spike. Properly derating the condenser and upgrading to 4-inch or 5-inch insulated panels avoids high-pressure cutouts during peak summer service.

Calculation Walkthrough

Texas High-Ambient Heat Load Formula

Governing Engineering Formula:
Total BTU/hr = Floor Sq Ft × Base Factor × ((Ambient 105°F − Box Temp) ÷ 60) × 1.18

Worked Example: 450 Sq Ft Restaurant Walk-In Cooler in Dallas, TX

  • Step 1 — High-Ambient ΔT: 35°F cooler box with 105°F summer design ambient → ΔT = 70°F.
  • Step 2 — Calculated Load: 450 sq ft × 30 × (70 ÷ 60) × 1.18 = 18,585 BTU/hr (1.55 Tons).
  • Step 3 — Condenser Selection: Select a 2.5 HP condensing unit rated to deliver >= 19,000 BTU/hr specifically at 105°F–110°F ambient.
  • Step 4 — Rooftop Placement: Elevate unit on 18-inch stands above roof membrane heat layer with clear airflow on all condenser coil sides.
Field Execution

Texas Permit & Compliance Checklist

STEP 01

Select From 105°F–110°F Catalog Column

Never size a Texas outdoor condensing unit using standard 90°F/95°F ratings.

STEP 02

Verify 16-to-18 Hour Daily Runtime

Size equipment so total 24-hour heat load is satisfied in 16 hours (coolers) or 18 hours (freezers with defrost).

STEP 03

Install Oversized Condenser Coil / Hail Guards

Protect rooftop fins from Texas spring hailstorms without restricting airflow.

STEP 04

Confirm Dedicated Electrical Disconnect

Provide weatherproof NEC disconnect and 120V GFCI service receptacle within 25 feet on the roof.

Technical Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions — Texas — ASHRAE High Ambient

What ambient temperature should I use to size a walk-in cooler in Texas?
Use at least 100°F–105°F for ground-level shaded units and 110°F for rooftop condensing units across Dallas, Austin, Houston, and San Antonio.
Why do walk-in coolers fail in Texas during July and August?
Undersized condensers selected at 95°F ratings cannot reject heat when rooftop air hits 110°F+, causing high head pressure trips and compressor overheating.
Do walk-in freezers in Texas need heated pressure relief ports?
Yes. Every commercial walk-in freezer requires a heated pressure relief port to equalize air pressure when warm humid air contracts after door openings.
What insulation thickness is best for Texas walk-in freezers?
Minimum 4-inch foamed-in-place polyurethane/PIR panels (R-32) for indoor freezers, and 5-inch panels (R-40) for outdoor boxes exposed to direct Texas sun.
